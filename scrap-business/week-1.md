# Week 1 - Foundation

**Date:** 24 May 2026

### What I Learned This Week
- Aluminum is the best metal to start with (low melting point, good demand, less fuel needed).
- Copper gives more profit but uses too much fuel.
- I need approximately 15–20 lakh to start small.
- EPA approval and proper chimney are mandatory.

### My Current Plan
- First buy a good insulated diesel furnace (target: 7–9 lakh)
- Start with 500kg–1 ton per day capacity
- Sell ingots in Lahore and Multan
