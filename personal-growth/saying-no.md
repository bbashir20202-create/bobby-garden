# Learning How to Say "No"

**Date:** 24 May 2026

### The Problem
My friends only see me as a "useful thing". When I say no, they call me selfish.

### What I'm Learning
- Saying no is not selfish. It's self-respect.
- People who call me selfish are emotionally manipulating me.
- I need to practice small "no"s first.

### My New Responses
- "Sorry, I can't do that right now."
- "I won't be able to help with that."
- "I understand you're upset, but I can't."

### Goal
Build the courage to say no without feeling guilty.

**Current Status:** Still very hard. But I'm trying.
